The Vision

Where Coastal Nature
Meets Architectural Elegance

Designed by Ashushri Creations for Biswajeet Ghosh at Balasore, Odisha, Anandamayee Eco Resort (AER) represents the pinnacle of sustainable luxury living.

Combining raw organic materials—weathered teak, clay roof tiles, and living botanical walls—with modern high-end architectural flourishes like cantilevered floating staircases, frameless floor-to-ceiling glass, and warm indirect LED cove illumination.
