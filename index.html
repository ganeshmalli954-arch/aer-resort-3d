Anandamayee Eco Resort is a thoughtfully designed beachside retreat that blends sustainable architecture with the raw beauty of nature.

Rear — Sea Breeze Deck

The back of each cottage opens to a private wooden deck, perfect for evening relaxation. The structure is elevated to catch the sea breeze and offers unobstructed views of the coastline.

West Side — Forest Canopy
